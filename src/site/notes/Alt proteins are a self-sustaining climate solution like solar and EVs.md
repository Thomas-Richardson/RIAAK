Alternative proteins are one of the few promising climate solutions because they are both market-based and science-based. Once the technology gets good enough, they sustain themselves. You do not need permanent subsidy or perpetual advocacy to keep them running. The market does that for you.

Compare it to solar. Once the solar market matured and became cheaper than fossil fuels, it became self-sustaining and profitable. It no longer needed to be propped up. The same happened with electric vehicles. Once they got good enough, with enough investment in the underlying science, they became a self-sustaining, profitable industry.

Alt proteins can do the same. Get the science right, get the products good enough, and the market takes over. Few other climate solutions can do that.
