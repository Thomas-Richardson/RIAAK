# Executive Summary / Abstract
[From Executive Summary]

- The 2026 Canada Animal Welfare Scorecard marks the fifth edition of the report, evaluating 46 leading companies on their cage-free egg and Better Chicken Commitment (BCC) policies.
- Approximately 80% of evaluated companies (37 of 46) are now publicly reporting progress on at least one animal welfare policy.
- The restaurant industry is a primary driver of progress, with 52% of evaluated restaurants now sourcing 100% cage-free eggs.
- Fifteen companies have fulfilled their cage-free policies, representing a 60% increase since the 2024 report cycle.
- A&W Canada, Boston Pizza, and Mary Brown’s Chicken fulfilled their cage-free egg commitments ahead of their original schedules.
- Canada remains an international laggard, with only 21% cage-free production compared with 82% in the United Kingdom and 47% in the United States.
- Restaurant Brands International became one of the first in its sector to publish a time-bound cage-free implementation roadmap that explicitly includes Canada.
- Major retailers including Save-On-Foods, Metro, Whole Foods, and IKEA have enhanced transparency by reporting progress against the Better Chicken Commitment.
- Sobeys has reported stagnant cage-free progress, remaining at approximately 17% for five consecutive years.
- Eight evaluated companies have weakened their commitments by accepting "enriched" cages as an alternative to true cage-free sourcing.
- Chairman’s Brands, parent company to 241 Pizza and Coffee Time, has never reported progress against its BCC policy despite operating nearly 500 locations.
- Research shows that 94% of Canadians believe it is important that animals’ physical and psychological needs are met.
- Multinational corporations often show higher standards elsewhere; for example, Costco reported 84.7% cage-free eggs in the U.S. but only 22.6% in Canada.
- The report argues that Canada's reliance on industry self-regulation has led to stagnation, suggesting a need for a national framework for farmed animal protection.
- Companies are urged to provide detailed, time-bound roadmaps and annual regional data to close the gap between commitments and implementation.
