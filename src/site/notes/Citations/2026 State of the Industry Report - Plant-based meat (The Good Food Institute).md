# Executive Summary / Abstract
[From Executive Summary]

- Global retail sales of plant-based meat, seafood, and dairy totaled an estimated $28.9 billion in 2025, a 3 percent increase from the previous year.
- Plant-based meat and seafood specifically accounted for $6.6 billion in global sales, though performance varied by region with growth in some areas and declines in others.
- The high-protein consumer trend continues to drive innovation, with major brands launching new high-protein milk blends and protein-dense product additions.
- Total investment in plant-based companies reached $450 million in 2025, up from $309 million in 2024, despite a generally tightening funding environment.
- The industry is experiencing accelerated consolidation, with at least 19 companies acquired as capital coalesces around scalable platforms and differentiated brands.
- Europe has emerged as a leader in the sector, driven by significant support from public and multilateral players like the European Investment Bank.
- Major food brands and retailers, including IKEA, McDonald’s, and Kraft Heinz, expanded their plant-based offerings with new menu items and unique retail formats.
- Scientific advancements in crop optimization and targeted breeding for pulses and legumes are helping to reduce downstream processing costs and improve nutritional value.
- Research into extrusion and texturization, such as in-line salt-solution injection, is successfully enhancing the fibrous structure and sensory performance of plant-based meats.
- Large-scale, open-access consumer sensory studies are providing critical data to help the industry achieve taste and texture parity with conventional animal products.
- National food strategies in countries like China, Denmark, and Germany are increasingly prioritizing plant-based foods to meet climate and food security goals.
- Regulatory hurdles remain a challenge, most notably in the European Union, where policymakers agreed to ban "meat" and related terms for plant-based products.
- Despite near-term economic turbulence, long-term growth is projected as the underlying need to diversify protein supply chains and address environmental pressures strengthens.
