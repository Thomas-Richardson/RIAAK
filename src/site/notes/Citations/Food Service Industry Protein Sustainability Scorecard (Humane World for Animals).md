# Executive Summary / Abstract
[From Key Takeaways]

- Among the top 50 food service management companies surveyed, 19 companies have plant-based commitments and eight companies have animal product reduction commitments.
- Seven companies received top performance grades of A+ or A: Guckenheimer, Elior North America, Metz Culinary Management, HHS, LLC, Sodexo, Fresh Ideas Food Service Management, and Aramark.
- Nine companies received grades of B+ or B, including Compass Group North America, Whitsons Culinary Group, Southwest Foodservice Excellence, and AVI Foodsystems, Inc.
- Guckenheimer exceeded its 55% plant-based commitment, reporting 56% plant-based meals in 2025.
- Metz Culinary Management achieved its goal of offering 50% plant-based meals by 2025 and is making progress toward reducing animal protein purchases by 10% by 2030.
- Elior North America successfully met its companywide goal (50% plant-based meals in new programs) and each of its three segment-specific goals by the end of 2025.
- Parkhurst Dining maintained its benchmark of 21%-25% plant-based menu options.
- OrganicLife and SLA Management set new goals to offer 33% plant-based meals by 2027 with complementary goals to reduce animal protein purchases by 2028.
- Seven companies that participated in previous scorecards failed to report this year, including American Dining Creations, Delaware North Companies, Inc., and Legends Hospitality.
- Creative Dining Services and Café Services Inc. received falling grades of D+ due to a lack of participation or shared supplemental data.
- More than 20 surveyed companies have yet to ever participate in this report, including HMSHost, OVG Hospitality, and Sage Dining Services.
