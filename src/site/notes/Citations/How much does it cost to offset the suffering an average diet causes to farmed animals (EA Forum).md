* The cost of offsetting the suffering a diet causes to farmed animals depends a lot on the question being asked. How much money is needed to help each type of farmed animal affected by the diet about as much as the diet harms it? Or how much money is needed to decrease the suffering of farmed animals as much as the diet increases it, regardless of which animals benefit? The cost also depends on the cost-effectiveness estimates used, and on how much they are adjusted for conservatism.
* [FarmKind](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/topics/farmkind)’s [Compassion Calculator](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/nGQRBWyCAbcEYSyLL/farmkind-using-a-diet-offset-calculator-to-encourage) answers the 1st question. It [recommends](https://www.farmkind.giving/compassion-calculator) donating [278](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1xfoqUE82r_dSJb1vqpmB39Ty1YYusUKAw3wbJTILgb8/edit?gid=845188512#gid=845188512&range=K9) $/person-year to offset the suffering random Western adult omnivores cause to farmed animals. It [models](https://www.farmkind.giving/blog/how-our-calculators-work) conservatively on purpose, as it wants donors to be confident that their donations are enough.
* For the 2nd question, I estimate 2.04 $/person-year for random people in the United States (US) based on [Animal Charity Evaluators](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/topics/animal-charity-evaluators)’ (ACE’s) results, and 0.279 $/person-year based on my own numbers for the suffering caused to farmed animals, and the past cost-effectiveness of the [Shrimp Welfare Project](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/topics/shrimp-welfare-project)’s (SWP’s) [Humane Slaughter Initiative](https://www.shrimpwelfareproject.org/humane-slaughter-initiative) (HSI).
* Changing one modelling choice at a time in FarmKind’s spreadsheet shows how much each choice affects the cost. For example, offsetting the suffering of birds raised for meat with cage-free corporate campaigns changes the cost from 278 to 109 $/person-year, and barely requires comparing welfare across species, as almost all birds raised for meat are chickens.
* Which estimate is more useful depends on what a donor wants an offset to do, and on how much margin they want for uncertainty.
