# Executive Summary / Abstract
[From Key Messages]

- In the EU, livestock systems contribute more than 65% of agricultural greenhouse gas emissions and are a major source of nitrogen pollution.
- Grazing and feed production account for more than half of total agricultural land use within the European Union.
- Addressing environmental pressures requires a rebalancing of livestock production and consumption patterns, particularly in feed-intensive systems, alongside continued sustainability improvements.
- European livestock systems rely heavily on substantial feed inputs and significant fertiliser use, creating a high dependence on imported feedstocks.
- This dependence extends the EU's environmental footprint globally and increases exposure to supply-chain disruptions, market volatility, and geopolitical risks.
- Diversification of the protein supply can significantly strengthen the resilience and security of European food systems.
- Key pathways for protein diversification include plant-based proteins, insects, biomass fermentation, precision fermentation, and cultivated meat.
- The sustainability performance of these alternative pathways depends on choices regarding land management, energy sources, and circular resource use.
- Boosting plant-based protein sources offers the most immediate environmental benefits for reducing greenhouse gas emissions, nitrogen pollution, and land-use pressures.
- Transitioning toward plant-based proteins creates opportunities for nature restoration and generates new value across farming and food systems.
- Protein diversification is expected to unfold gradually and complement, rather than rapidly replace, livestock production.
- Measures are required to avoid market concentration and ensure the affordability of new protein sources as the market balance evolves.
- Managing labor-market impacts is essential to support a just transition, particularly in regions that are currently livestock-dependent.
- Coherent governance and sustainability safeguards can ensure that diversification strengthens the resilience and competitiveness of Europe's food systems.
- Targeted regional support and strategic priorities should align protein diversification with overarching climate and biodiversity goals.
