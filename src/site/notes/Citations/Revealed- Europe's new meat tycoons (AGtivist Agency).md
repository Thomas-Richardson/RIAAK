- A handful of corporations now dominate the supply of meat, eggs and dairy in parts of Europe, raising concerns about power and influence, consumer choice and the spread of industrial farming.
- New research spanning the UK, Spain, and Italy found that meat, eggs, and dairy products are increasingly produced by large corporations using industrial farming methods. For consumers, the risk is that cost savings become profits, not lower prices, even as payments to farmers are squeezed.
- Key findings include:
  - **UK:** Almost complete market domination of poultry supply by just three companies; eight businesses control about 70% of meat processing turnover.
  - **Spain:** Seven companies control half the pork slaughtering business; five account for nearly half of total dairy turnover.
  - **Italy:** Five companies control half the poultry, pig, and beef meat supply chain from slaughtering onwards.
