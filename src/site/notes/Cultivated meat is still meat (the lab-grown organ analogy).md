A framing for arguing that cultivated meat is genuinely meat, not an imitation or substitute.

If you grow an ear or a liver in a lab for an organ transplant, it is still an organ. If you take a cutting from a plant and grow a new plant, it is still a plant. In the same way, cultivated meat is still meat.

The point is that where and how the cells grow does not change what they are. The same logic applies to meat grown from animal cells outside the animal's body.
