## Dutch hybrid cheese pizzas

In the Netherlands there is a significant category of pizzas topped with a cheese that mixes dairy cheese and plant-based cheese. This blend is unusual and worth noting as its own product category.

## Price-driven reformulation toward vegan

Many companies add real butter and eggs to baked goods. When the price of these ingredients goes up, they substitute them out. So we may find more products becoming naturally vegan over time, not because of any vegan intent but as a side effect of ingredient cost.
