We should not count out alternative proteins just because some organisations are going bust. Early failures in a sector do not mean the sector will fail.

When cars were first being developed, around 500 car companies failed, including Henry Ford's first company. Ford went on to build one of the most successful companies in history. The early wave of failures did not mean cars were a dead end.

During the dot-com bubble, Amazon lost 98 percent of its value and people wondered when Jeff Bezos would ever turn a profit. It obviously bounced back. The collapse in valuation said nothing about whether the underlying business or the wider sector had a future.

The same logic applies to alt proteins. Companies going bust is what early, capital-intensive sectors look like. It is a sign of a shakeout, not a sign that the whole category is doomed.
