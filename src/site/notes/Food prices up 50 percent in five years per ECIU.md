# Food prices up 50 percent in five years per ECIU

A report from the Energy and Climate Intelligence Unit (ECIU) about food prices forecasted that in just five years, from 2021 to 2026, food prices will have increased by 50 percent. That same level of food price inflation took the previous 19 years to occur.
