Countries with a higher Human Development Index tend to have more political parties that mention animal welfare. The higher the HDI, the more parties bring it up.

This is a correlation, not evidence of a causal link. It does suggest animal welfare gets more political attention as a country develops, which is worth keeping in mind when thinking about where party-level advocacy is likely to gain traction.
