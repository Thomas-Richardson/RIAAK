# The Good
- Targets people who are against factory farming, who'd never go vegan
- Raises awareness of factory farming among heavy meat eaters.
- Uses role models that would never support Veganuary
- Most people have heard of Veganuary however almost no one is familiar with the concept of offsetting animal product consumption. This campaign basically uses the rare publicity of Veganuary to boost the awareness of donating.
- Animal movement needs donations more than new Vegans
- Animal movement needs to try new things
- If we can set up a "two pronged Veganuary" where people either go vegan or donate, that could make January a whole-population thing.
- There's going to be backlash to Veganuary, we might as well make money off it.

# The bad
- Damages Veganuary's reputation, esp because of Toni
	- But would it have got attention if it wasn't her? 
- Are we ok as a movement with one org damaging another org for their own gain? 
	- It's not FarmKind's gain.
- Offsets become somewhat problematic when most people do them, but we're miles away from that
- Could increase meat consumption for some people who choose to eat 
- Is seen as infighting
	- But if its agreed among parties that it's not infighting is it an issue? 
- Risk that some people who were going to do Veganuary donate instead.
	- Risk is acute for people who'd have become vegans for a while
	- But actually donating for Veganuary is more effective than trying Veganuary
