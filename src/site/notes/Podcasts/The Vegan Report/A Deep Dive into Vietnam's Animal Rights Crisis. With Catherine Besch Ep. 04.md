Vietnam Animal Aid & Rescue is an organization in the front line of fighting for animal rights. The dog and cat meat trade, bad veterinary care, and a growing consumption of animal products are some of the issues this vegan sanctuary is working on resolving. Join Rayane and Catherine Besch, founder and director of Vietnam Animal Aid, in discussing the dire state of animal welfare in Vietnam.
