In French, work or job translates to travail and travail stems from the word tripalium, which was a medieval torture instrument consisting of three sticks to which you were tied in order to receive a beating. Needless to say for many cultures, working meant suffering.

But nowadays, things are different. There is a variety of jobs out there with different levels of pleasantness. Still, it is rare to come across people who are truly passionate about their jobs, who find meaning in their job, the kind of job that aligns with your most deeply held convictions, a job that makes a difference, a true positive impact in the world.

If you are vegan and looking for that kind of job, then this episode is for you since we will be talking with Ben Bramman, the founder of veganjobs.com.
