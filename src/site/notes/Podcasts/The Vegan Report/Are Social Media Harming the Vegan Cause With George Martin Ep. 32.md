Animal rights organizations tend to be hyperactive on social media and this is nothing to say of the army of vegan influencers out there. Yet, is it the right way to go? Can't we be just a little bit critical of this proximity with social media, and self-reflect on what it means for veganism on the long-term?

To discuss this topic, I have with me someone who is not afraid of playing with daring ideas. A public commentator and vegan influencer, whose insights and critical thinking I find valuable. His name is George Martin, but you also know him as Carnism Debunked.
