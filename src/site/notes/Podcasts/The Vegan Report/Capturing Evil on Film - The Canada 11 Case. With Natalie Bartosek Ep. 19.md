If you are listening to this podcast, chances are, you have watched undercover footage of how farm animals are treated. Those pictures and videos are violent, choking, and can even be traumatizing. But what is certain is that those images never leave you indifferent. They force you, for a moment, to face an evil which has been actively hidden from your sight.

Well, have you ever wondered how that footage is produced? Who is behind the camera? And what does it cost to capture a photo of that ugliness? Join Natalie and Ryan in answering those questions!
