I believe we all want to see a world where veganism is the norm. But, sadly, opportunities to explore critically our different perspectives and beliefs in reaching that goal are rare.

So, for today, in the name of fostering critical thinking, I decided to gather three influential vegans to debate three important questions:

What are the most and least effective ways to advance the vegan cause?

Should we advocate for veganism or reducing animal products consumption?

Should vegans exclusively lead the animal rights cause?
