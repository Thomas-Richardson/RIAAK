Salmon, Shrimps and canned tuna are the most consumed seafood around the world. In terms of vegan alternatives, there is a variety of mock meat options in the case of beef, pigs or chickens. But seafood seems to be the exception. Good vegan seafood products are kind of rare to find. But that trend is changing for the better. BY2048, a Montreal based startup offering plant-based smoked salmon, is part of that change. Join Ryan and Arthur, the founder of BY2048, in discussing mass fishing, the Future Ocean Foods initiative, going further in your vegan transition, what it takes to be an entrepreneur, and more!
