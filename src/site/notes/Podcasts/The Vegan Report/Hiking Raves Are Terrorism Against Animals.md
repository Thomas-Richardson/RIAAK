The Party Scientist is an entrepreneur, an event planner who has organized (might still be operating) many hiking raves. I discovered him on Instagram, where he regularly promotes his hiking raves through multiple accounts. He opened my eyes on the new trend of hiking raves, a form of animal cruelty I wish to denounce.

My message to Jacques:

Hi Jacques,

My name is Rayane, and I am messaging you today about the hiking raves you organize.

I am happy for your success. However, can you please consider that your forest raves are disturbing countless animals and insects?

Human-generated sounds negatively impacts animals by interfering with communication, foraging, navigation, and reproduction, potentially leading to stress, habitat abandonment, and even injury or death.

By avoiding loud music or screams in the forest, you are providing a safe and peaceful environment for innocent beings who struggle to survive.

Maybe your groups could wear headphones or organize those raves in the artificial environment of a greenhouse or glasshouse.

I hope this message finds you well.

Thank you,

Rayane
