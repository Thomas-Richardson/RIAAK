In this episode, we examine a new survey commissioned by the UK nonprofit Project Phoenix, revealing that the majority of Britons think the country should eat less meat, that most want factory farming to be phased out, and that many want it gone fast.

Together, we discuss why most people can't wrap their heads around the scale of factory farming, when our activism should be bold and ambitious, and why some people told surveyors that factory farms were perfectly acceptable. I mean, are they psychopaths or something? And much more.
