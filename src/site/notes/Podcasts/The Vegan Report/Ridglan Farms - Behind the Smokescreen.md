In this episode, you will hear the unfiltered truth about the Ridglan Farms protests. By now, you have surely heard about this so-called Beagle rescue. It has attracted immense media attention and sympathy from the animal rights movement. And my suspicion is that if you ask any vegan about it, they will tell you that it is the story of a triumphant group of activists closing down one of the worst dog breeding facility in the US and rescuing thousands of dogs from a terrible fate.

But what if everyone had it wrong? What if the story and reporting you heard on this issue missed the mark and by a mile, and the Ridglan Farms protests did not save dogs, they actually harmed dogs, harmed activists, and harmed this movement as well.

To expose the truth on this issue, I have with me Dr. Crystal Heath, founder of Our Honor, the only organization empowering veterinarians to oppose the abuse of the animal agriculture industry.

She has been on the frontline of covering this story and she joins me now for an exclusive conversation discussing what happened at Ridglan Farms, why this event has taken the proportions it has, the hate against animal lab researchers and meat-eaters we see being legitimized, how could that hate lead to mass violence, the chances of the arrested protesters to escape prison, and much much more.
