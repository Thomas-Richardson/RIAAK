The Vegan Festival of Montreal might be the best way to convince someone of considering a vegan lifestyle. Far away from obtuse social media arguments, participants are exposed to the full richness of veganism: from its delicious gastronomy, to the infectious passion of animal rights activists. Join Ryan and Louis-Alexandre Gagnon, founder & president of The Vegan Festival of Montreal, in discussing the making of this full-scale event.
