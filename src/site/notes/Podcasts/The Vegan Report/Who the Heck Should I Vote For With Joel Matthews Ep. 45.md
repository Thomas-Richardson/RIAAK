Have you ever been disappointed by our political class? Thinking that our dearly held values of compassion, as vegans, were simply not represented. Feeling politically homeless.

Well, today let's confront those sentiments. Let's have an open conversation on the left, the right, and the place of veganism in that political theater.

To discuss this topic, I have with me Joel Matthews. Joel is a writer and academic preparing to publish a book called Human Supremacy, which is a fascinating reflection on the role of religion and western culture in our exploitation of animals. He has thought deeply about society and often shares his insights on social media.
